# 26 hook formulas

Contents: how to use, the table (templates in Russian, original wording), formulas to avoid.

Pick 3 formulas per topic, write one hook per formula, score all, keep the best two. Replace `{...}` with something real and checkable.

## Avoid
- Formulas 4, 23 and 25 with made-up numbers; any "I earned $X" claim you cannot show.
- Clickbait that the video does not pay off in the first 5 seconds.
- Copying another creator's exact words. Formulas are shapes, not sentences.

Formula shapes are common creator craft. The idea of collecting them, and the five-check scoring idea, were inspired by the MIT-licensed `Jakeschincariol/instagram-agent-skill`; wording and code here are our own.
