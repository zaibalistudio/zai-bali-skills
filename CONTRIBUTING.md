# Contributing

Issues are welcome: a bug, a wrong claim in a skill, a missing trigger phrase, a better rule. Please include the request you typed, what the skill did, and what you expected.

Pull requests: open an issue first so we agree on the change. Keep a skill's `SKILL.md` under 500 lines, `name` in lowercase kebab-case, `description` under 1024 characters (what it does and when to use it, third person). Do not add personal data, keys, local paths or anything that publishes on a user's behalf. Claims need a label: documented, tested by you, or hypothesis.

Answers may be in English or Russian. Expect a reply within a few days.
